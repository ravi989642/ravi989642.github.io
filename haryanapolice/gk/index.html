Haryana GK (हरियाणा सामान्य ज्ञान)
Haryana Budget & NSDP
According to the Haryana Budget Structure and Fiscal Responsibility Act, the Tertiary Sector (Service Sector) contributes the most to Haryana's Net State Domestic Product (NSDP). This includes banking, government jobs, private jobs, and teaching services.

Tertiary Sector > Primary > Secondary in current Haryana NSDP

Per Capita Income of Haryana
Haryana's Per Capita Income is HIGHER than the national average. Reason: Smaller area + high tax revenue from Gurgaon & Faridabad companies. Don't confuse: High per capita income does NOT mean every person is rich — it is a statistical average.

Haryana's per capita income > National Average (asked in CET Group D)

Aravali Biodiversity Park, Gurugram
Located in Gurugram. India's FIRST OECM site (Other Effective Area-based Conservation Measure) by IUCN. NOT on Sahibi River basin — it's in the urban area of Gurugram.

OECM = protects biodiversity without being a formal national park

Aravali Peaks & Highest Points
Aravali's Highest Peak: Dosi ki Pahadi (652m) — located in Kultajpur village, Mahendragarh. It is the tapobhumi of Chavan Rishi.

Haryana's Highest Peak: Karo Choti (1514m per HSSC) in Shivalik range.

Dosi ki Pahadi = 652m (Aravali) | Karo Choti = 1514m (Shivalik)

Jawaharlal Nehru Canal (Lift Irrigation)
Jawaharlal Nehru Canal is a LIFT irrigation system from Western Yamuna Canal. It serves Mahendragarh, Rewari, and Bhiwani. Started in 1976 during drought, expanded by Chaudhary Devi Lal. Called "Lift" because Aravalli hills are higher — water must be lifted up.

Lift Irrigation = water lifted to higher Aravalli terrain

Western Yamuna Canal (Oldest & Biggest)
Haryana's oldest & biggest canal. Taken from Yamuna River at Hathnikund Barrage / Tajewala (Yamunanagar). Construction started in 1355 by Firoz Shah Tughlaq. Called "Father of Canals in Haryana." Later maintained by Akbar and British.

Firoz Shah Tughlaq = Father of Canals in Haryana (1355)

Bhakra Canal
From Nangal Dam on Sutlej River (Punjab). Enters Haryana at Tohana, Fatehabad. Serves Hisar, Fatehabad, Sirsa area. Tohana is called "City of Rivers".

Tohana = City of Rivers | Bhakra = Nangal Dam, Sutlej River

Gurugram Canal
Taken from Yamuna River at Okhla Barrage, Delhi. Serves Gurugram and Faridabad districts.

Okhla Barrage (Delhi) → Gurugram Canal → Gurugram + Faridabad

Other Lift Canals
Jui Canal: Bhiwani — Lift Irrigation
Indira Gandhi Canal / Loharu Lift Canal: Bhiwani & Charkhi Dadri
Rule: Wherever you see "Lift" in South Haryana canals = Aravalli hills area (higher terrain).

"Lift" = South Haryana (Mahendragarh, Rewari, Bhiwani)

Haryana's Taj Mahal — Sheikh Chilli's Tomb
Located in Thanesar, Kurukshetra. Called "Haryana's Taj Mahal" by British officer Davis Ross. Sheikh Chilli was the guru of Dara Shikoh (Mughal era).

Thanesar = Thane + Sar (Police station) → Remember: Belt padne pe chillana = Chilli!

Haryana's Qutub Minar — Kazi ki Masjid
Located in Dujana village, Jhajjar. Design similar to Qutub Minar. Called "Haryana's Qutub Minar".

Dujana = Jhajjar | Jhajjar = Kharaab haalat → haath dhujenge = Dujana

Haryana's Lal Kila — Sheesh Mahal
Located in Farrukhnagar, Gurugram. Built in 1732 by Faujdar Khan. Called "Haryana's Lal Kila" because built with same red stones as Delhi's Lal Kila.

Sheesh = Glass (rough nature) → Farrukhnagar = Rukha (rough)

Karn ka Tila, Gaurav ka Talab, Karn Jheel
Karn ka Tila: Thanesar, Kurukshetra (where Karn died in Mahabharat)
Gaurav ka Talab: Rohtak (Gau = cow, cow crying = Rohtak)
Karn Jheel: Karnal (Karn + Jheel = Karnal)

Karn ka Tila = Kurukshetra | Gaurav Talab = Rohtak | Karn Jheel = Karnal

History — Satavahana Dynasty & Revolutions
Satavahana Dynasty — Basics
Founder: Simuka
Capital: Pratishthan / Paithan (present Aurangabad, Maharashtra)
Early Capital: Amaravati (Andhra Pradesh)
Language: Prakrit | Script: Brahmi (Brahma → Prakriti)
Special: Matriarchal society, Land donation practice, Lead coins issued.

Lead (Shisha) coins = Only Satavahana dynasty issued them!

Satakarni I (First Powerful King)
First powerful king of Satavahanas. Titles: Dakshinapthapati (Lord of the South) & Apratihat Chakra. Performed 2 Ashwamedh Yagyas and 1 Rajasuya Yagya. Wife: Naganika — information from Nanaghat Inscription.

Nanaghat Inscription → Naganika (wife of Satakarni I)

King Hala — Gatha Saptashati
Wrote Gatha Saptashati — a collection of 700 love poems in Prakrit language.

Hala = 17th king, wrote Gatha Saptashati (love poetry collection)

Gautamiputra Satakarni (Most Powerful)
23rd king, most powerful Satavahana ruler. Defeated Shak king Nahapana. Titles: Eka-Brahmana (Unique Brahmin) & Vedashram. His mother Gautami wrote Nasik Prashasti about him. First to use mother's name before his own (matrilineal tradition).

Nasik Prashasti = Written by mother Gautami | Defeated Nahapana (Shak)

Geography — Earth's Interior & Discontinuities
Earth's Crust (Bhuparpati)
Outermost thin solid layer. Depth: Ocean = 5 km, Continent = 30-70 km. Made of two parts:
SIAL = Si (Silicon) + Al (Aluminium) — Upper Crust
SIMA = Si (Silicon) + Mg (Magnesium) — Lower Crust

SIAL = Si + Al (Upper) | SIMA = Si + Mg (Lower)

Mantle
Below crust. Covers 84% of Earth's volume. Depth: 2900 km. Made of only SIMA. Asthenosphere = Upper Mantle's semi-fluid part (source of lava/volcanoes).

Mantle = 84% of Earth | Asthenosphere = Semi-fluid lava source

Core (Center of Earth)
Deepest layer. Total Earth radius = 6371 km. Made of NIFE = Ni (Nickel) + Fe (Iron).
Outer Core = Liquid (source of Earth's magnetic field)
Inner Core = Solid (due to extreme pressure)

NIFE = Ni + Fe | Outer = Liquid (Magnetic field) | Inner = Solid

Economics — Cost Concepts
Marginal Cost (Seemant Laagat)
Marginal Cost = Change in total cost when ONE additional unit is produced.

Example: 100 pens cost ₹200, 110 pens cost ₹220 → Marginal cost for 10 extra pens = ₹20.

Graph: Marginal Cost curve is U-SHAPED.

MC = Change in Total Cost / Change in Quantity | U-Shaped Graph

Total Cost = Fixed + Variable
Total Cost = Fixed Cost + Variable Cost

Fixed Cost: Rent, permanent salaries, factory lease — does NOT change with production.

Variable Cost: Raw material, electricity bill, temporary expenses — changes with production level.

Fixed = Permanent (Rent, Salary) | Variable = Changes (Raw material, Electricity)

Average Cost
Average Cost = Total Cost ÷ Total Units Produced

Example: ₹200 for 100 pens → Average Cost = ₹2 per pen.

Average Cost = Total Cost / Number of Units
